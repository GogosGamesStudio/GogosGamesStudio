Discord Server Rules

Welcome! Follow these rules to keep the community friendly, safe and organised. Rule-breaking may result in warnings, mutes, kicks, bans or permanent blacklisting depending on severity.

01 🤝 Be Respectful

No harassment, bullying, discrimination, threats, personal attacks or excessive toxicity.

02 🔞 No NSFW / Inappropriate Content

No sexual, graphic, disturbing or otherwise inappropriate content. Keep the server safe for the community.

03 🚫 No Weird / Inappropriate Emojis

Do not use emojis or reactions in a sexual, offensive, insulting, disturbing or intentionally disrespectful way.

04 🤡 No Insulting Reactions

Do not use clown, middle-finger, skull, laughing or similar reactions specifically to mock or insult another member.

05 💬 No Spam or Flooding

No message, emoji, GIF, reaction or mention spam, repeated images, excessive caps or unnecessary flooding.

06 📢 No Advertising / Promoting

Do not advertise Roblox games, Discord servers, social pages, YouTube/TikTok accounts, websites, products or services without staff permission.

07 💸 No Scamming

Scamming, phishing, fake giveaways, account stealing or intentionally deceiving members may result in an immediate ban.

08 🔗 No Malicious Links or Files

Do not send suspicious links, executables, downloads or files that may harm another user's device or account.

09 📌 Do Not Ping the Owner

Do not ping the server owner unless it is genuinely important or you have specifically been asked to.

10 📩 Do Not DM the Owner

Do not privately message the owner asking for roles, Robux, partnerships, free items, support or attention. Repeated unnecessary DMs may result in a blacklist.

11 🙏 Do Not Beg for Roles

Do not repeatedly ask for Tester, Moderator, Admin, Builder, Developer or other roles. Use the official application system.

12 🔔 No Unnecessary Staff Pings

Only ping staff when you genuinely need help. Do not repeatedly ping multiple staff members for the same issue.

13 📂 Use Channels Correctly

Keep conversations in the correct channels and follow any channel-specific rules or pinned instructions.

14 🎭 No Impersonation

Do not impersonate staff members, developers, creators, content creators or other members.

15 🔒 No Leaking Private Content

Do not leak unreleased updates, tester content, development information, staff conversations, screenshots, files or private assets.

16 💻 No Exploiting or Cheating

Do not share, request, advertise or use exploits, cheats, scripts or methods intended to unfairly affect our Roblox games.

17 🚨 No False Reports

Do not submit fake reports, fabricated evidence or intentionally waste staff time.

18 🌍 English Only

Please use English in public chat channels so the moderation team can properly monitor the server.

19 ⚖️ Respect Staff Decisions

If you disagree with a moderation action, contact staff respectfully. Do not create public arguments or drama.

20 📜 Follow Discord & Roblox Rules

Discord's Terms of Service, Community Guidelines and Roblox's applicable rules must be followed at all times.

⚠️ Important: Attempting to bypass punishments with alternate accounts may result in a permanent blacklist. Repeated rule-breaking, staff harassment, excessive owner DMs/pings, leaking content, scams or serious misconduct may result in an immediate permanent blacklist without additional warnings. Use common sense — not every possible situation can be written into a rule.

✅ By joining and remaining in the server, you agree to follow these rules.
